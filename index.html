Our Philosophy

We treat every garden as architecture that grows.

Founded in 2009 by a landscape architect and a hydro-irrigation engineer, PRANA began with a simple frustration: gardens on the Costa del Sol were either imported wholesale from Northern Europe, or left to fight the Andalusian summer alone. We build the third way — planting schemes drawn from the region's own hills and coastline, engineered with the water discipline the climate demands.

Sixteen years on, our studio still keeps design, planting and irrigation under one roof — so the plan on paper is the garden that survives August.

    Our Specialism

    Lawns, engineered for Andalusian summers

    Lawns are where PRANA began, and where our engineering runs deepest. Every lawn we lay is chosen, drained and irrigated for the specific microclimate of your plot — not a catalogue default.

    01

    Natural & artificial turf

    Drought-resistant warm-season varieties — Bermuda, Zoysia, Paspalum — for full sun, and premium artificial turf for shaded terraces, rooftops and heavy-shade courtyards.

    02

    Smart irrigation & water conservation

    Weather-linked automatic systems, drip lines for beds, and soil-moisture sensors that cut water use — designed within current Junta de Andalucía restrictions.

    03

    Seasonal maintenance

    Scheduled mowing, core aeration, overseeding and a two-stage fertilisation programme timed to the Mediterranean growing calendar, not a generic quarter.
