Our Philosophy

"A society grows great when old men plant trees whose shade they shall never sit in."

Three of them arrived at this together — an architect from Iraq, an engineer from Finland, and a woman from Ukraine who asked only one question: what will remain? They met at the World Circular Economy Forum. Between them, decades of work across four continents. They understood how the world worked. That was precisely the problem.

In 2022, they came to the Costa del Sol with one shared obsession: a garden should do something to you. Stop you. Slow your breath. Make you forget, for a moment, that you had somewhere else to be.

PRANA was born from that. Not a business plan. A feeling they refused to leave behind.

Prana. The breath inside all living things. We simply refuse to waste it.

    Our Specialism

    Lawns, engineered for Andalusian summers

    Lawns are where PRANA began, and where our engineering runs deepest. Every lawn we lay is chosen, drained and irrigated for the specific microclimate of your plot — not a catalogue default.

    01

    Site preparation & grading

    Soil analysis, precise grading and a drainage plan worked out before a single roll of turf goes down — the groundwork that decides whether a lawn thrives or fails.

    02

    Natural & artificial turf

    Drought-resistant warm-season varieties — Bermuda, Zoysia, Paspalum — for full sun, and premium artificial turf for shaded terraces, rooftops and heavy-shade courtyards.

    03

    Smart irrigation & water conservation

    Weather-linked automatic systems, drip lines for beds, and soil-moisture sensors that cut water use — designed within current Junta de Andalucía restrictions.

    04

    Overseeding & renovation

    Core aeration, overseeding and targeted repair that bring a tired, patchy or drought-stressed lawn back to dense, even green — often without a full re-turf.

    05

    Seasonal maintenance

    Scheduled mowing at the right height for each season, plus a two-stage fertilisation programme timed to the Mediterranean growing calendar — not a generic quarter.
